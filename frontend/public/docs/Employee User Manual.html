Chapter 1

Getting Started

Welcome to anix HRMS — your all-in-one Human Resource Management System, designed exclusively for the anix family. This manual will guide you through every feature available to you as a Employee, helping you make the most of the application from day one.

1.1   Self-Registration via Onboarding

Your journey begins with self-registration. When your account is being set up, you’ll receive a link to the Onboarding page where you can complete your registration independently.

  1. Navigate to the Onboarding page using the link provided by your Admin or HR.
  2. Fill in your personal details — full name, email address, phone number, and department.
  3. Create a secure password that you’ll use for all future logins.
  4. Submit the form. Your information is securely saved and your account is created.
The Onboarding page where you complete your self-registration

1.2   Account Activation via Email

After successful registration, an activation email is sent to the email address you provided. Open the email and click the activation link to verify your account. This step ensures your identity and activates your HRMS profile.

💡
Tip If you don’t see the activation email in your inbox, check your Spam or Junk folder. The email is sent from the anix HRMS system automatically.

1.3   First Login

After clicking the activation link in your email, you may be automatically signed in and taken directly to your Dashboard. This is normal — the system recognizes you from the activation and skips the login step.

If you happen to log out or close the browser before completing your first session, you can always return to the login page and sign in manually with your email and password.

The anix HRMS login screen
ⓘ
Note If your account is locked after multiple failed login attempts, contact your Admin to unlock it and reset your password.

1.4   Changing Your Password

For security, it’s a good practice to change your password periodically. You can do this anytime from the sidebar.

  1. Click the dropdown arrow next to your name in the sidebar.
  2. Select Change Password.
  3. Enter your current password, then your new password.
  4. Click Update to save the changes.
⚠
Security Reminder Choose a strong password with a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Avoid using easily guessable information like your name or date of birth.

Chapter 3

My Profile

The My Profile section is your personal hub where you can view and manage your information, upload important documents, and keep your profile up to date.

The My Profile page — your personal information at a glance

3.1   View & Edit Personal Details

You can view and update the following personal information:

Field Description
Full Name Your official name as registered with anix
Phone Number Your contact number for official communication
Address Your current residential address
Email Your registered email address (read-only after registration)

To update your details, click the Edit button, make your changes, and save. Some fields may require HR approval before changes take effect.

3.2   Default Week-Off Day

Your profile displays your default week-off day (e.g., Sunday). This is the day you’re regularly off each week. If you need to change this, contact HR (or Admin if HR is not available).

3.3   Upload Documents

You can securely upload and manage important documents directly from your profile:

📄

ID Proof

Upload a copy of your government-issued ID (Aadhaar, Passport, etc.)

📄

PAN Card

Upload your PAN card for tax and payroll processing

📄

Bank Passbook

Upload your bank passbook or statement for salary crediting

🔒
Privacy & Security All uploaded documents are stored securely. Your HOD has view access to your documents, while HR and Admin have full access for management purposes. Your data is handled with the utmost confidentiality.

If you need to update or re-upload any document (such as a renewed ID proof or updated PAN card), please contact HR (or Admin if HR is not available).

3.4   Email Verification Status

Your profile shows whether your email address has been verified. A verified email ensures you receive all important notifications, leave approvals, and system alerts. If your email is not yet verified, check your inbox for the verification link.

❗
Important Always provide a correct and verified email address when updating your profile. If you change your email to an incorrect or inaccessible address, you may permanently lose access to your account and data with no way to restore it. Double-check before saving any email changes.

Chapter 4

Attendance

anix HRMS uses geolocation-based attendance to ensure accurate and transparent tracking. Your attendance is validated against your allocated office, giving you confidence that your presence is recorded fairly.

The Attendance page with geolocation-based check-in

4.1   Geolocation-Based Check-In

When you arrive at the office, you can check in using your device’s GPS:

  1. Open the Attendance page from the sidebar.
  2. Click the Check In button. The system will request access to your browser’s GPS.
  3. Allow location access when prompted by your browser.
  4. The system verifies your location against the registered office coordinates.
  5. If you’re within the designated office area, your check-in is successfully recorded.
⚠
Important Ensure you are at your allocated office and that location services are enabled in your browser before checking in. Your attendance location is verified against your assigned office.

4.2   Monthly Calendar View

The Attendance page features a visual monthly calendar that gives you a complete overview of your attendance history. Each day is color-coded to indicate your status:

Present

Days you successfully checked in and out.

Absent

Days with no attendance record.

Approved Leave

Days where your leave has been fully approved.

Week-Off

Your regular weekly off-day (e.g., Sunday).

4.4   Real-Time Attendance Sync

Your attendance data is synced in real time with the HRMS database. This means:

  • Your check-in time is recorded instantly.
  • Your attendance history is always up to date across all devices.
  • HR and your HOD can see your attendance status in real time.

Chapter 5

Leaves

The Leaves module is your complete leave management hub. From applying for leaves to tracking their approval status, everything you need is organized into four convenient tabs.

5.1   Request Leave Tab

This is where you apply for leave. The process is simple and intuitive.

The Request Leave tab — apply for leave in just a few steps

How to Apply for Leave

  1. Navigate to Leaves → Request Leave tab.
  2. Select the Leave Type from the dropdown (Sick, Casual, Earned, etc.).
  3. Choose your leave duration: Full Day or Half Day.
  4. If Half Day, select First Half or Second Half.
  5. Pick the start date and end date (for multi-day leaves).
  6. Enter a reason for your leave in the text area.
  7. Click Submit to send your request for approval.
ⓘ
Leave Types Available The leave types available to you will be configured by HR as per your organization’s policy. The available types will appear in the dropdown when you apply.

5.2   Leave Balance Tab

Keep track of your leave entitlements at a glance. The Leave Balance tab provides a clear breakdown of your leave quota.

View your leave consumption — Used days against Allocated days for each leave type

Understanding the Leave Balance Table

Each row in the Leave Balance table represents a leave type available to you. The columns show your leave consumption — how many days you’ve used out of the total allocated.

Column Description
Leave Type The category of leave (e.g., Sick, Casual, Earned)
Allocated Total number of days allocated for the year
Used Number of days you have already utilized
Available Remaining days you can still apply for (Allocated − Used)

5.3   My Requests Tab

Track the status of all your leave requests in one place. The My Requests tab gives you complete visibility into the approval process.

Track your leave requests with the visual progress bar

Approval Workflow

Every leave request follows a two-step approval process:

1
Applied
2
HOD Approval
3
HR Approval
  • Applied — Your request has been submitted and is awaiting review.
  • HOD Approval — Your Head of Department has reviewed and approved the request.
  • HR Approval — HR has given final approval. Your leave is now confirmed.

Click any request row to view its full details, including timestamps, approver comments, and rejection reasons (if any).

💬
Need a Reminder? If your request has been pending for a while, you can reach out to your HOD (or HR if it has reached HR) directly to request a review.

5.4   Calendar History Tab

The Calendar History tab provides a visual, color-coded month view of all your leaves and week-offs. It’s a quick way to see your leave patterns over time.

Color-coded calendar showing leaves and week-offs at a glance
🔎
Clickable Calendar Cells Each calendar cell is clickable. Clicking on a day with a leave request will take you directly to that request in the My Requests tab, where you can view its full details.

Approved Leave

Leaves that have been fully approved by HOD and HR.

Pending Leave

Leaves that are awaiting approval.

Week-Off

Your regular or swapped weekly off-day.

Rejected / Cancelled

Leaves that were rejected or cancelled.

5.5   Leave Request Detail Popup

Clicking any leave request opens a detailed popup with complete information about that request.

Detailed view of a leave request — dates, approvers, timestamps, and comments

The popup displays:

  • Leave Type — The category of leave applied for
  • Leave Dates — Start and end dates of the leave
  • Duration — Full day or half day (with First/Second Half indicator)
  • Reason — The reason you provided when applying
  • Approver Names — Who reviewed your request at each stage
  • Timestamps — When the request was applied, approved, or rejected

5.6   Cancel a Leave Request

Plans change, and anix HRMS lets you cancel a pending leave request if needed.

  1. Go to the My Requests tab.
  2. Click the request you want to cancel to open the detail popup.
  3. If the request is still Pending, you’ll see a Cancel Request button.
  4. Click Cancel Request and confirm your decision.
⚠
Note You can only cancel requests that are in Pending status. Once a request is approved by your HOD or HR, it cannot be revoked from the system. In such cases, please contact your HOD or HR directly.

Chapter 6

Week-Off Swap

One of the most useful features in anix HRMS is the Week-Off Swap. It gives you the flexibility to swap your regular weekly off-day with a working day — perfect when you need a specific day off that isn’t your usual week-off.

The Week-Off Swap dialog — swap your off-day with a working day

6.1   How It Works

Let’s say your default week-off is Sunday, but you need Tuesday off next week. With Week-Off Swap, you can swap Sunday for Tuesday — working on Sunday and taking Tuesday off instead.

📅

Default Week-Off

View your current default week-off day (e.g., Sunday) as set in your profile.

🔒

Swap Window

The system shows you when the swap window is open — the period during which you can request a swap.

✅

Apply for Swap

Select the working day you want as your new off-day and submit the swap request.

6.2   Applying for a Week-Off Swap

  1. Navigate to the Week-Off Swap section from the sidebar.
  2. View your current default week-off day and the swap window dates.
  3. Select the date you want to swap your week-off to.
  4. Review the details and click Apply.
  5. Your request is sent to your HOD for approval.

6.3   Conflict Detection

The system is smart about preventing scheduling conflicts. If you already have a leave request on the date you’re trying to swap to, you’ll receive a warning.

❌
Conflict Detected! You have an existing leave on [date]. You have two options:
  • Cancel the conflicting leave to proceed with the swap.
  • Choose a different date for your swap.

6.4   Swap Status Tracking

Once you’ve applied for a swap, you can track its status:

Status Meaning
Available You haven’t applied for a swap yet. The swap option is open.
Swap Applied Your swap request has been submitted and is awaiting HOD and HR approval.
Swap Consumed The swap has been approved and the off-day change has been applied.
⚠
Reminder Your HOD and HR must both approve the swap before the swap date. Apply well in advance to ensure timely approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

1 How do I change my default week-off day?
Contact your HR (or Admin if HR is not available) to modify your default week-off. This cannot be changed from the Employee portal directly.
2 My account is locked due to multiple failed login attempts. What do I do?
Contact your Admin to unlock your account and reset your password. For security reasons, locked accounts can only be unlocked by authorized personnel.
3 How do I apply for a half-day leave?
Go to Leaves → Request Leave tab. Select “Half Day” and choose either First Half or Second Half from the options.
4 Can I cancel my leave request after applying?
You can cancel your request from the My Requests tab, but only while it is still Pending. Once your request has been approved by both your HOD and HR, it cannot be cancelled through the system. In that case, please contact your HOD or HR directly.
5 How does the system know my attendance location?
Your browser’s GPS is used to verify you are within the office premises. Ensure location access is enabled in your browser settings when checking in.
6 How do I upload my documents?
During onboarding, you can upload your ID proof, PAN card, and bank passbook in the documents section. All uploads are encrypted and stored securely. If you need to update or re-upload any document after onboarding, please contact HR (or Admin if HR is not available).
7 What is a Week-Off Swap?
A Week-Off Swap lets you swap your regular weekly off-day with another working day. For example, if your default off is Sunday but you need Tuesday off, you can swap them. Your HOD must approve the swap first, followed by HR approval, before the swap date takes effect.
8 Who can see my leave requests?
Your HOD can see and approve/reject your requests. HR and Admin can also view them for organizational visibility and record-keeping.
9 How do I view my leave balance?
Go to Leaves → Leave Balance tab. You’ll see allocated, used, and available days for each leave type in a clear table.
10 I applied for leave but haven’t heard back. What should I do?
Your request is likely awaiting HOD approval. You can reach out to your HOD directly to request a review. If it has already been approved by HOD and is pending with HR, contact your HR team.
