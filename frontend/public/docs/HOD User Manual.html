Section 1

⚙ Sevak Features Overview

As an HOD, you have full access to all Sevak features. The sections below provide a brief summary. For complete details, refer to the Sevak User Manual.

💡
HOD = Sevak + More

Every feature available to a Sevak is also available to you. This section briefly covers those features. The HOD-specific features that extend beyond the Sevak role are covered in detail in Sections 2–4.

🔒 1.1 Getting Started & Login

Access the anix HRMS application through your organisation’s login page. Enter your registered email address and password, then click Sign In. If you enter incorrect credentials three times, your account will be temporarily locked for 30 minutes. Use the Forgot Password? link to reset your password via email.

The anix HRMS login page

🏠 1.2 Dashboard

After logging in, you land on the Dashboard. It displays a daily wisdom quote from Gurudev, along with quick-access cards for attendance, leaves, and other key features. The dashboard gives you an at-a-glance summary of your activity and pending items.

Dashboard with Gurudev’s Wisdom and quick-access cards

👤 1.3 My Profile

View and manage your personal information under My Profile. This includes your name, contact details, department, designation, employment type, and default week-off. You can also update your profile picture and change your password from this page.

My Profile page with personal and employment details

📅 1.4 Attendance

Mark your daily attendance using the Geo Check-In feature, which verifies your location. The attendance page shows your check-in and check-out times, total hours worked, and a calendar view of your monthly attendance. You can also view your attendance history and filters by date range.

Attendance page with geo-based check-in and check-out

📅 1.5 Leaves

The Leaves section has four tabs: Request Leave (apply for new leave), Leave Balance (view available leave types and balances), My Requests (track your leave request history with a visual progress bar), and Calendar History (see approved leaves on a calendar view). Each request goes through an approval workflow: Sevak → HOD → HR.

Request Leave tab

Leave Balance tab

My Requests with progress tracking

Calendar History view

🔄 1.6 Week-Off Swap

If you need to swap your regular week-off day, you can submit a Week-Off Swap request. Select the week-off day you want to swap with, provide a reason, and submit. The request is sent to your HOD for approval. You must submit the request before the original week-off date.

Week-Off Swap request dialog

🚪 1.7 Logout

To log out, click on your profile icon in the top-right corner and select Logout. This securely ends your session and returns you to the login page. Always log out when using shared devices.

Section 2

👥 Sevak Directory (Department View)

As an HOD, you can view and manage all sevaks within your assigned department.

🔍 View Department Members

The Sevak Directory gives you a complete list of all sevaks (employees) in your department. You can see their names, designations, contact details, attendance status, and leave summaries at a glance. Use the search bar to quickly find a specific sevak by name, or use the filter options to narrow results by designation, employment type, or status.

📄 View Individual Profiles

Click on any sevak’s name to view their detailed profile. From their profile, you can access:

  • Personal Information — Name, email, phone, date of birth, etc.
  • Employment Details — Department, designation, employment type, date of joining
  • Attendance Logs — Daily check-in/out records, total hours, and attendance history
  • Leave Summary — Leave balances across all leave types and recent leave history
⚠
Department Restriction

HODs can only view sevaks in their own assigned department. You do not have visibility into sevaks from other departments. This ensures data privacy and proper role-based access control.

📊 Use Cases

  • Team Overview — Quickly check who is available today and who is on leave
  • Onboarding Verification — Confirm that a new sevak’s profile is complete
  • Attendance Monitoring — Review attendance patterns for your department members
  • Leave Planning — Assess team capacity before approving leave requests
Section 3

✅ Leave Approvals

The primary HOD responsibility: reviewing and acting on leave requests from sevaks in your department.

✔
This is Your Key HOD Feature

As an HOD, you are the first level of approval for leave requests from sevaks in your department. Once you approve, the request moves to HR for final approval. Your timely action ensures sevaks receive responses quickly.

HOD Pending Approvals page — your main workspace for leave management

📋 3.1 Pending Requests

When you navigate to Leave Approvals, you land on the Pending tab by default. This tab shows all leave requests from your department sevaks that are awaiting your approval. Each request card displays:

  • Sevak Name — Who is requesting leave
  • Leave Type — Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Earned Leave, etc.
  • Dates — Start and end dates of the requested leave
  • Duration — Full day or half day (with period: first half / second half)
  • Reason — The sevak’s stated reason for the leave
  • Applied On — When the request was submitted

Leave Request Detail dialog with full information and action buttons

🔍 3.2 Leave Request Detail

Click on any pending request to open the Leave Request Detail dialog. This dialog provides the complete picture:

Field Description
Requester Name and designation of the sevak
Leave Type The category of leave (Casual, Sick, Earned, etc.)
From – To Leave date range
Duration Full day, First Half, or Second Half
Reason The sevak’s explanation for the leave
Status Current workflow status (Pending HOD / Pending HR / Approved / Rejected)
Progress Bar Visual indicator showing where the request is in the approval flow

At the bottom of the dialog, you have three action buttons:

✔ Approve

Approve the leave request with one click. The request moves to HR for final approval.

✘ Reject

Reject the request. You must provide a reason or comment explaining the rejection.

🔔 Notify

Send an email reminder to the sevak about their pending request.

✅ Approving a Request

Click Approve to approve the leave. You can optionally add a comment before approving (e.g., “Approved. Enjoy your time off!”). Once approved, the request status changes to Pending HR and moves to HR for final approval. The sevak will be notified of your approval.

❌ Rejecting a Request

Click Reject to deny the leave request. You must provide a reason or comment when rejecting — this is mandatory and helps the sevak understand why their request was denied. The sevak will be notified of the rejection and can choose to reapply.

📃 3.3 Tab-Based Filtering

The Leave Approvals page is organized into tabs for easy navigation:

Tab What It Shows
Pending Requests awaiting your approval (default tab)
Approved Requests you have already approved
Rejected Requests you have rejected
Cancelled Requests that were cancelled by the sevak
All Complete history of all requests from your department
💡
Search & Filter

Use the search bar to find requests by sevak name. You can also filter by leave type, date range, or status to quickly locate specific requests.

🔔 3.4 Notify & Follow-Up

The Notify button is a powerful follow-up tool. When you click it, the system sends an email notification to the sevak reminding them about their pending leave request. This is useful when:

  • A sevak has submitted a request but hasn’t provided required documentation
  • You need the sevak to clarify or update their reason
  • The request has been pending for a while and you want to nudge the sevak
💡
Best Practice

Use Notify before rejecting a request. Give the sevak a chance to clarify or update their request. This promotes a supportive and communicative work environment.

⏩ 3.5 HOD Skip

In certain situations, you may need to bypass your own approval and send a leave request directly to HR. The Skip HOD option allows you to do this. When you use this feature:

  1. The leave request skips the HOD approval step entirely
  2. The request is sent directly to HR for final approval
  3. The request status updates to Pending HR
⚠
When to Use HOD Skip

Use this feature sparingly and only when truly necessary — for example, when you are going to be unavailable for an extended period and the leave request is urgent, or when you have a conflict of interest (e.g., the request is from a close family member working in your department).

Section 4

🔄 Week-Off Swap Approvals

Approve or reject week-off swap requests from sevaks in your department.

📋 How It Works

When a sevak submits a week-off swap request, it appears in your Week-Off Swap Approvals queue. You can review the request details including the original week-off day, the requested swap day, and the reason provided by the sevak.

✅ Approve or Reject

Review the swap request and choose to Approve or Reject. If rejecting, provide a reason so the sevak understands the decision. Once approved, the sevak’s week-off day is updated for the specified period.

⚠
Important: Approve Before the Swap Date

You must approve the week-off swap request before the original week-off date. If you do not approve it by then, the request auto-cancels and the sevak keeps their original week-off day. There is no way to approve an auto-cancelled request — the sevak would need to submit a new request.

🕑 Timeline Example

Suppose a sevak’s default week-off is Sunday, and they request to swap it to Wednesday for a specific week:

  • You must approve the swap before Sunday of that week
  • If approved: the sevak works on Sunday and is off on Wednesday
  • If not approved by Sunday: the request auto-cancels, and the sevak is off on Sunday as usual

Quick Reference: Approval Deadlines

Week-off swap approvals must be completed before the original week-off date. Late approvals are not possible — the request auto-cancels. Plan ahead and review swap requests promptly.
