Overview

Welcome to the anix HRMS HR User Manual. This manual is your comprehensive guide to using the Human Resource Management System developed by anix.

Your Role: HR

As an HR user, you have full organizational visibility and administrative control. You can manage all employees across every department, oversee leave workflows, maintain attendance compliance, manage departments, and communicate with the entire workforce. You also have access to everything a Employee and HOD can do.

What You Can Do

👤

Manage All Employees

View the complete organizational directory, onboard new members, manage profiles, and handle account lifecycle.

📋

Leave Administration

Oversee all leave requests organization-wide, approve or reject leaves, manage leave types, and configure policies.

📊

Attendance & Compliance

Monitor attendance logs across the organization, track geo-mismatch violations, and generate compliance reports.

🏢

Department Management

Create and manage departments, assign HODs, and configure office locations for each department.

📧

Bulk Communication

Send official communications to employees by role, department, or location with recipient preview and acknowledgement.

Section 2

HR-Specific Features

These features are exclusive to the HR role. They provide organization-wide visibility and administrative control over the people operations of anix.

2.1 Employee Directory (Full View)

The Employee Directory gives HR a complete, organization-wide view of all employees — not limited to a single department. This is the central hub for managing the entire workforce.

🔑 Key Difference from HOD View HODs can only see employees in their department. HR sees all employees across all departments with advanced search, filtering, and bulk actions.

Directory Tabs

The directory is organized into three tabs for efficient management:

👥

All Employees Default

View every employee in the organization. Search by name, employee ID, email, role, or department. Filter results to find specific employees quickly.

🆕

Onboardings Financial Month Filter

View newly registered employees grouped by financial month. This helps track recent joiners and ensure their onboarding is complete. Use the financial month selector to filter by specific periods.

⏸️

Inactive > 3 Months

Identify employees who have been inactive for more than 3 months. Use this tab to decide whether to reactivate or archive these accounts.

Actions Per Employee

Each employee entry in the directory provides the following actions:

Action Description
View Profile Open the employee's full profile with personal, employment, and organizational details
Leave Summary View leave balances, history, and pending requests for the employee
Attendance Log Review the employee's attendance records including geo-validation status
Deactivate / Activate Temporarily disable or re-enable a employee's account access
Delete Request / Withdraw Submit a deletion request for inactive accounts, or withdraw a pending request before Admin acts on it
⚠️ Important Deactivating a employee revokes their login access but preserves their data. Activate anytime to restore access. You can submit a delete request for INACTIVE accounts and withdraw it anytime before Admin/SuperAdmin acts on it. Activating an account automatically clears any pending delete request.

Add New Employee

Invite new members to the organization directly from the Employee Directory:

  1. Click the "Add New Employee" button in the directory header
  2. Enter the new employee's email address — an invitation will be sent
  3. Fill in required details: name, department, designation, and role
  4. Set the initial week-off day and reporting manager
  5. Click "Send Invitation" — the employee receives an email to complete registration
💡 Tip The new employee will appear in the "Onboardings" tab until their onboarding is complete. You can track their registration status from there.

CSV Export

Export the directory data for offline analysis or record-keeping:

  1. Apply any desired filters (department, role, status)
  2. Click the "Export CSV" button
  3. A CSV file will be generated with all matching records
  4. The file includes: Name, Employee ID, Email, Department, Role, Status, Joining Date

Modify Leave Balances

During onboarding, HR can adjust leave allocations for a new employee. This is a one-time action available only on the activation day.

🔒 Restriction Leave balance modification is allowed only once, on the employee's activation day. After that, balances follow the standard annual cycle. Plan adjustments carefully.
  1. Go to Employee Directory → Onboardings tab
  2. Find the employee and click "Modify Leave Balances"
  3. Adjust the balances for each leave type as needed
  4. Click "Save" — the adjustment is recorded and the option is locked

Financial Month Filtering for Onboardings

The Onboardings tab includes a financial month selector to help you track new joiners within specific periods. This is especially useful during the April onboarding rush when many new employees join the organization.

2.2 Leave Admin

The Leave Admin module gives HR full visibility and control over leave management across the entire organization. Unlike the HOD view (limited to their department), HR can see and act on all leave requests from every department.

HR Leave Admin page with organization-wide leave visibility

Leave Admin Tabs

⏳

Pending Requests Action Required

Shows all leave requests that need attention. This tab contains two types of requests:

  • PENDING — Waiting for HOD approval. Use the Notify HOD button to send email reminders.
  • HOD_APPROVED — Already approved by HOD, ready for your (HR) action. Click Approve or Reject.
📋

All Requests

Complete history of every leave request across the organization. Search and filter by employee name, department, role, leave type, or status. Each request shows a visual progress bar indicating where it is in the workflow.

⚙️

Manage Types

Create, edit, and delete leave types (e.g., Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Earned Leave). Configure default balances, carry-forward rules, and eligibility criteria.

Manage Leave Types

Configure the leave types available to employees:

Operation How Notes
Create Click "Add Leave Type", fill in name, code, default balance, and rules New types are immediately available for selection
Edit Click the edit icon on any leave type Changes apply to future allocations only
Delete Click delete, enter admin password to confirm Only types with zero usage can be deleted. Password protection prevents accidental deletion.
⚠️ Password-Protected Deletion Deleting a leave type requires entering the admin password. This safeguard prevents accidental deletion of leave types that may be in use.

Approval Workflow

Leave requests follow a structured two-level approval process:

Employee Applies
→
HOD Reviews
(PENDING)
→
HR Reviews
(HOD_APPROVED)
→
Approved / Rejected
📧 Notify HOD If a leave request has been in PENDING status for too long, click the "Notify HOD" button to send an email reminder to the respective HOD. This helps prevent delays in the approval process.

Each leave request in the list includes a visual progress bar showing exactly where it is in the workflow — from submission to final approval/rejection.

2.3 Departments

Manage the organizational structure by creating, editing, and deleting departments. Each department can have an assigned HOD, office locations, and a member list.

Departments management page

Department Management Features

➕

Create Department

Click "Add Department" and enter the department name. Optionally assign an HOD and office locations during creation.

✏️

Edit Department

Modify department name, reassign HOD, or update location assignments. Changes take effect immediately.

🗑️

Delete Department

Remove a department that no longer exists. Only departments with no active members can be deleted.

Assign HOD to Department

When creating or editing a department, you can assign a Head of Department. Only employees with the EMPLOYEE role are eligible for HOD assignment. The HOD will receive pending approval notifications for leave and week-off requests from their department.

Office Location Assignment

Assign one or more office locations to each department. Employees in that department will use the assigned location for geo-validated attendance check-in. Location assignment includes an interactive map for visual confirmation.

💡 Tip Departments with no assigned HOD will have their leave requests routed directly to HR for approval. Always ensure every active department has an HOD assigned.

2.4 Attendance Reports

Monitor and manage attendance compliance across the entire organization. The Attendance Reports module provides comprehensive visibility into attendance logs, geo-validation, and compliance metrics.

HR Attendance Reports with compliance overview

Key Features

📊

Attendance Compliance

View all attendance logs across the organization with geo-validation flags. Identify missed check-ins, late arrivals, and early departures at a glance.

📍

Geo-Mismatch Logs

A dedicated view for location violations — instances where a employee checked in from outside their designated work location. Review and take appropriate action.

✏️

Manual Attendance Update

HR can add or edit attendance records manually to correct errors or account for exceptional situations (e.g., system outages, remote work).

🔄

Real-Time Sync via SSE

Attendance data updates in real-time using Server-Sent Events (SSE). No page refresh needed — new records appear automatically as employees check in/out.

Monthly Compliance Report

The report includes summary cards and a detailed breakdown:

Summary Cards

  • Total Records — Number of attendance entries for the selected period
  • Missed Attendance — Days where employees did not check in
  • Geo Mismatch — Number of location validation failures

Below the summary cards, a employee-wise breakdown table shows individual attendance records with details like check-in time, check-out time, total hours, and geo-validation status.

Report Controls

  • Month/Year Selector — Choose the reporting period
  • Department Filter — View reports for specific departments
  • CSV Download — Export the report for offline analysis or archival

2.5 Official Communication (Bulk Communication)

Send official emails to employees across the organization with flexible targeting options. This feature allows HR to communicate important announcements, policy changes, or event information to specific groups or the entire workforce.

Official Communication dialog for composing bulk emails
Accessing Official Communication from the Employee Directory

Targeting Modes

Mode Description Use Case
All Employees Send to every active employee in the organization Organization-wide announcements
By Department Select one or more departments as recipients Department-specific updates
By Role Multi-select roles (Employee, HOD, HR, Admin) Role-based communications
Custom Include or exclude specific employee IDs Targeted messages to individuals or small groups

Exclude Field

All targeting modes include an Exclude field. Use this to remove specific employees, departments, or roles from the recipient list. This is useful when you want to send to "everyone except a specific group."

How to Send a Bulk Communication

  1. Navigate to Employee Directory and click the "Official Communication" button
  2. Select the targeting mode (All, Department, Role, or Custom)
  3. Configure recipients using the available filters
  4. Optionally add entries to the Exclude field
  5. Enter the Subject and compose your Message
  6. Click "Preview Recipients" to verify the recipient list before sending
  7. Review the count and click "Send"
  8. An acknowledgement popup confirms the send with the total recipient count
💡 Tip Always preview recipients before sending to ensure you're communicating with the right audience. The acknowledgement popup provides a final confirmation with the exact recipient count.

2.6 Week-Off Maintenance (Settings)

Access the Week-Off Maintenance feature from the Settings page to manage stale week-off swap requests.

Auto-Cancel Stale Week-Off Swaps

Over time, week-off swap requests may become stale — these are requests where the swap date has already passed but the request was never approved or rejected. The auto-cancel feature cleans up these stale requests.

  1. Navigate to Settings from the sidebar
  2. Find the "Week-Off Maintenance" section
  3. Click "Auto-Cancel Stale Week-Off Swaps"
  4. The system will identify and cancel all swap requests where the swap date has passed
  5. A confirmation message shows how many requests were cancelled
ℹ️ Note This action only affects swap requests where the requested swap date is in the past. Pending requests for future dates are not affected.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers specific to the HR role in anix HRMS.

1. How do I change a employee's default week-off day?
Go to Employee Directory, find the employee, click Edit Profile, and modify the default week-off day. The change takes effect from the next scheduling cycle.
2. How do I approve leave requests that are waiting for HOD?
Go to Leave Admin → Pending Requests tab. Requests with PENDING status need HOD action first — use the Notify HOD button to send a reminder. Requests with HOD_APPROVED status are ready for your action (Approve/Reject).
3. Can I modify a employee's leave balance after onboarding?
Only on the activation day, and only once. After that, balances follow the standard annual cycle. Plan any adjustments carefully during the initial onboarding period.
4. How do I create a new department?
Go to Departments → click "Add Department". Enter the department name, assign an HOD (only EMPLOYEE-role employees are eligible), and optionally assign office locations with interactive map support.
5. How do I send a bulk email to all employees?
Go to Employee Directory → click "Official Communication" button. Select recipients using targeting modes (All, Department, Role, or Custom), compose your message, preview recipients, and send. An acknowledgement popup confirms the recipient count.
6. Can I see attendance for all employees?
Yes. Go to Attendance Reports for a comprehensive view with geo-validation flags, monthly compliance summaries, employee-wise breakdowns, and CSV export. Use the month/year selector to choose the reporting period.
7. How do I handle a delete request?
In the Employee Directory, find the INACTIVE employee and click "Delete Request" from the actions menu. This flags the account for Admin/SuperAdmin review. If you change your mind, click "Withdraw Request" (same location) to cancel it. Note: activating an account automatically clears any pending delete request.
8. What if HOD doesn't approve leaves for a long time?
Use the "Notify HOD" button in Leave Admin → Pending Requests to send email reminders to the respective HOD. You can also contact the HOD directly through official channels. Persistent non-response should be escalated to administration.
9. How do I change my own default week-off?
Contact the Admin to modify your default week-off day. As HR, you cannot modify your own settings — this is a security measure to prevent unauthorized self-modification.
10. My account is locked. What do I do?
Contact the Admin to unlock your account and reset your password. Account locks typically occur after multiple failed login attempts. The Admin can unlock your account and set a new password for you.
11. What is Week-Off Maintenance?
Go to Settings → Week-Off Maintenance and click "Run Now" to manually trigger the auto-cancel process. This cancels any pending leave requests that overlap with the upcoming week-off days for all employees. It is normally run automatically, but you can trigger it manually if needed.
