PART 1
Welcome to anix HRMS

Welcome to the anix HRMS (Human Resource Management System) — a purpose-built application by anix (Art of Living) to manage the complete employee lifecycle of Sevaks across the organization.

This SuperAdmin User Manual is the comprehensive guide to the entire application. It covers every feature available to every role — from a Sevak checking in for the day, to a SuperAdmin configuring system-wide settings. If you need to know how anything works in anix HRMS, it is documented here.

What This Application Does

anix HRMS is a centralized platform for managing:

  • Attendance — Geo-validated check-in/check-out tied to office locations
  • Leave Management — Apply, approve, track, and report on leaves with full workflow
  • Week-Off Management — Default week-offs, swap requests, and approval workflows
  • Employee Directory — Complete Sevak profiles with documents, departments, and roles
  • Official Communication — Bulk email to individuals, departments, or role-based groups
  • Account Administration — Create, lock, unlock, reset, and delete accounts
  • Office Locations — Manage physical office locations with GPS coordinates and geo-thresholds
  • System Configuration — SMTP, attendance reminders, geo thresholds, and more

PART 2
Getting Started

Self-Registration (Onboarding)

New Sevaks can register themselves through the Onboarding page. This is typically the first interaction a new user has with the system.

Onboarding page — Self-registration form for new Sevaks
  1. Navigate to the Onboarding Page
    Click the "Register" or "New Sevak" link on the login page, or visit the onboarding URL directly.
  2. Fill in Personal Details
    Enter your full name, email address, phone number, and any other required fields such as department or date of joining.
  3. Submit the Form
    Click the submit button. An activation email will be sent to the provided email address.

Account Activation

After self-registration, an activation email is sent to the registered email address.

  1. Check Your Inbox
    Look for an email from anix HRMS with the subject "Account Activation" or similar. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.
  2. Click the Activation Link
    The email contains a link that will activate your account and allow you to set your password.
  3. Set Your Password
    Create a secure password. Once set, your account is active and ready to use.

Login

Access the application through the login page using your registered email and password.

Login page — Enter your email and password to access anix HRMS
  1. Enter Your Credentials
    Type your registered email address and password into the respective fields.
  2. Click "Sign In"
    If your credentials are correct, you will be redirected to the Dashboard. If your account is locked, you will see an error message.
⚠️ Account Lockout

If you enter the wrong password too many times, your account will be locked automatically. Contact your Admin or SuperAdmin to unlock it.

Changing Password

You can change your password at any time from the My Profile page. This is a security best practice — change your password periodically.

  1. Navigate to My Profile
    Click on your name or profile icon in the navigation bar, then select "My Profile".
  2. Click "Change Password"
    Find the Change Password section on the profile page and click the button.
  3. Enter Current & New Password
    Type your current password, then your new password twice to confirm.
  4. Save Changes
    Click "Save" to update your password. You may be asked to log in again.

Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you land on the Dashboard — your home base for the application.

Dashboard — Gurudev's Wisdom quote, highlights, and quick statistics

The Dashboard provides:

  • Gurudev's Wisdom — A unique daily quote from Gurudev Sri Sri Ravishankar
  • Quick Stats
  • Highlights
  • Navigation — Quick links to all major sections of the application

PART 4
Sevak Features

These are the core day-to-day features used by every Sevak — checking attendance, applying for leaves, and managing week-off swaps.

Attendance — Geo-Based Check-In

anix HRMS uses geo-validated attendance. When you check in, your browser's GPS coordinates are compared against your assigned office location. If you are outside the configured radius, your attendance is flagged as a "Geo Mismatch" for HR review.

Attendance — Geo-based check-in with live GPS status
Attendance — Calendar view of attendance records

How Geo-Validation Works

  1. Click "Check In"
    When you arrive at the office, click the Check In button on the Attendance page. Your browser will request GPS access.
  2. GPS Coordinates Captured
    Your current latitude and longitude are captured from your browser's Geolocation API.
  3. Comparison Against Office Location
    The system calculates the distance between your GPS coordinates and your office location. If the distance exceeds the configured threshold (in meters), your attendance is flagged.
  4. Result
    If within range: "On Time" or "Present". If outside range: "Geo Mismatch" — flagged for HR review but not blocked.
⚠️ Geo Mismatch

A geo mismatch does not prevent you from checking in. It flags the record for HR review. If you are working remotely or at a different office, inform your HR in advance.

  3. Submit Request

Week-Off Swap

Need to work on your usual day off and take a different day off instead? Use the Week-Off Swap feature.

Week-Off Swap — Select a working day to swap with your default week-off

How Week-Off Swap Works

  1. Navigate to Leaves
    Go to the Leaves section and find the "Week-Off Swap" area.
  2. Select a Date
    Choose a working day that you want to swap with your default week-off. The system will check for conflicts.
  3. Submit the Request
    Your swap request is sent to your HOD for approval. If approved, that day becomes your week-off, and your default day becomes a working day.
  4. HOD Approval
    The HOD must approve the swap before the swap date. If the swap date has already passed, the request is automatically cancelled.
ℹ️ Conflict Detection

The system automatically detects conflicts — if you already have a leave or another pending swap for the selected date, you will be notified and the swap cannot be submitted.

HOD Features

Heads of Department (HODs) have all Sevak features plus department-level management capabilities.

Department Directory

HODs can view all Sevaks within their own department. This provides visibility into team members, their profiles, and their leave status.

  • View list of all Sevaks in your department
  • See each Sevak's profile details
  • Monitor pending leave requests from your team
  • View attendance summaries for your department

Leave Approvals

HODs are the first level of approval for leave requests from Sevaks in their department.

HOD Pending Approvals — Review and act on team leave requests

Approval Workflow

  1. Receive Notification
    When a Sevak in your department submits a leave request, it appears in your Pending Approvals queue.
  2. Review the Request
    Click on the request to see full details — leave type, dates, reason, and the Sevak's leave balance.
  3. Approve or Reject
    Click "Approve" to forward the request to HR, or "Reject" with remarks. The Sevak is notified of your decision.
  4. Skip HOD Option
    If the HOD is unavailable, the system allows the request to skip HOD approval and go directly to HR. This is indicated in the approval chain.
⚠️ HOD Skip

If you will be unavailable, inform your HR so they can process requests from your department directly. The "Skip HOD" option ensures leaves are not delayed.

Week-Off Swap Approvals

HODs also approve week-off swap requests from Sevaks in their department. The process is similar to leave approvals — review the swap date, check for conflicts, and approve or reject.

Admin Features

Admins manage user accounts — creating new accounts, handling lockouts, resetting passwords, and processing delete requests.

Account Management

The Admin dashboard provides a comprehensive view of all accounts in the system.

Admin Account Management — All accounts, locked accounts, and delete requests

Three Tabs

Tab Purpose
All Accounts Complete list of all registered accounts with status, role, and last login
Locked Accounts Accounts locked due to failed login attempts — unlock with one click
Delete Requests Accounts pending deletion — review, revoke, or permanently delete (SuperAdmin only). "Requested By" shows who raised the request.

Add Account (OTP Flow)

Admins can create new accounts for HR personnel or other Admins through an OTP-verified flow.

  1. Click "Add Account"
    On the Account Management page, click the "Add Account" button.
  2. Enter Email and Role
    Provide the new user's email address and assign a role (HR or Admin). An OTP is sent to the provided email.
  3. Verify OTP
    The user must provide the OTP received via email to verify their identity.
  4. Account Created
    Once OTP is verified, the account is created and the user receives login credentials.

Lock / Unlock Accounts

Accounts are automatically locked after too many failed login attempts. Admins can unlock them:

  • Go to the "Locked Accounts" tab
  • Find the locked account
  • Click "Unlock" — the account is immediately accessible again

Reset Password

Admins can reset any user's password. The user receives a new temporary password via email and is prompted to change it on next login.

Delete Account

When HR submits account deletion requests, they appear in the "Delete Requests" tab. The "Requested By" column shows who raised the request. Admins and SuperAdmins can:

  • Revoke — Cancel the delete request without deleting the account
  • Delete Permanently — Permanently delete the account and all associated data (SuperAdmin only)
ℹ️ Auto-Clear on Activation

Activating an account (changing status from INACTIVE to ACTIVE) automatically clears any pending delete request for that account.

⚠️ Permanent Action

Account deletion is irreversible. All data associated with the account will be permanently lost. Verify the request before approving. You can Revoke the request if you change your mind before deletion.

SuperAdmin Locations — Create, edit, and manage office locations with interactive maps

Field Description

System Settings

The Settings page is the control center for the entire application. Only SuperAdmins can access and modify these settings.

SuperAdmin Settings — Geo threshold, SMTP, attendance reminders, and system configuration

Geo Validation Threshold

Configure the default maximum distance (in meters) that a Sevak can be from their office location during check-in before their attendance is flagged as a "Geo Mismatch."

  • Default: Typically set to 100–500 meters depending on office location
  • Per-Location Override: Each location can have its own threshold configured in Locations Management
  • Effect: If a Sevak checks in from a distance greater than this threshold, their attendance record is flagged for HR review
ℹ️ Threshold Behavior

The threshold does not block check-in — it only flags the record. This allows Sevaks working from nearby locations or in the field to still check in, while providing HR with visibility into attendance patterns.

Mail Configuration (SMTP)

Configure the email system used for all official communications, password reset links, and notifications.

SMTP Settings

Setting Description
Official Communication Email The "From" email address used when HR/Admin sends bulk emails
SMTP Server Your SMTP server address (e.g., smtp.gmail.com, smtp.office365.com)
SMTP Port Port number (typically 587 for TLS, 465 for SSL)
SMTP User Username for SMTP authentication
SMTP Password Password for SMTP authentication (stored securely)
From Name Display name in outgoing emails (e.g., "anix HRMS")
From Email Reply-to email address
Password Reset Link Validity How long (in hours/minutes) a password reset link remains valid

Testing Your Configuration

  1. Enter SMTP Details
    Fill in all SMTP fields with your email provider's settings.
  2. Click "Send Test Email"
    The system sends a test email to the configured address to verify the SMTP connection works.
  3. Verify Receipt
    Check your inbox for the test email. If received, your SMTP configuration is correct. If not, check your credentials and server settings.
⚠️ Security

SMTP passwords are stored securely in the database. Never share your SMTP credentials with anyone. If you suspect they are compromised, update them immediately in Settings.

Attendance Reminder

Configure automated attendance reminders to ensure Sevaks check in on time.

Reminder Settings

  • Enable/Disable Toggle — Turn attendance reminders on or off system-wide
  • Send Reminder Now — Manually trigger an immediate reminder to all Sevaks who haven't checked in today
  • Deadline Time Display — Shows the configured deadline by which Sevaks should check in
  • Last Sent Date — Tracks when the last reminder was sent for auditing
✅ Best Practice

Enable attendance reminders and set the deadline to your standard office start time. This helps Sevaks remember to check in and reduces the number of manual attendance corrections HR needs to make.

Week-Off Maintenance

The auto-cancel feature for stale week-off swaps ensures that pending swap requests where the swap date has already passed are automatically cancelled.

How It Works

  • Lazy Execution — The auto-cancel runs automatically on API calls (when someone loads the leaves page, etc.)
  • Manual Trigger — You can manually trigger it from Settings → Week-Off Maintenance
  • Effect — Any pending week-off swap where the swap date is in the past is automatically cancelled and the Sevak is notified
ℹ️ When to Use Manual Trigger

Use the manual trigger if you notice a large number of stale swap requests building up, or before generating attendance reports to ensure data accuracy.

All System Configurations

The Settings page also provides a view of all system configuration keys and their current values. This is useful for:

  • Auditing — See every configuration value at a glance
  • Troubleshooting — Quickly identify misconfigured settings
  • Advanced Customization — Edit configuration keys directly (use with caution)
⚠️ Caution

Editing system configuration keys directly can break application functionality. Only modify values you fully understand. When in doubt, contact the development team.

System Data Reset

The most powerful and dangerous feature in the system. Located in My Profile → Danger Zone.

🔴 DANGER — System Data Reset

This action is irreversible. System Data Reset will wipe all application data including Sevak records, leaves, attendance, departments, and configurations. Only use this when absolutely necessary (e.g., testing, data migration, or system overhaul).

Before proceeding:

  • Export all data you need to preserve
  • Notify all users that the system will be reset
  • Take a full database backup
  • Ensure you have the database credentials to restore from backup if needed
PART 9
Frequently Asked Questions
How do I change a Sevak's default week-off?

SuperAdmin Admin: Go to Sevak Directory → find the Sevak → Edit Profile → modify default week-off.

HR: Go to Sevak Directory → find the Sevak → Edit Profile → modify.

HOD Sevak: Contact HR (Admin if HR unavailable) to modify.

My account is locked. What do I do?

Sevak HOD HR: Contact Admin or SuperAdmin to unlock.

Admin: Contact SuperAdmin to unlock.

SuperAdmin: Another SuperAdmin can unlock your account, or you can use an alternate SuperAdmin account.

How do I change my default week-off day?

Sevak HOD: Contact HR (Admin if HR unavailable).

HR Admin SuperAdmin: Go to Sevak Directory → your profile → Edit → modify default week-off.

How do I apply for a week-off swap?

Go to Leaves → find the Week-Off Swap section → select a date → submit. Your HOD must approve the swap before the swap date. The system automatically detects conflicts with existing leaves or pending swaps.

Can I approve my own leave request?

No. Sevak/HOD requests go through the HOD → HR approval chain. Your own requests always need higher-level approval. No one can approve their own leave in the system.

How do I send a bulk email?

HR Admin SuperAdmin: Go to Sevak Directory → Official Communication → select recipients → compose and send.

You can filter recipients by role, department, or manually select individuals.

How does attendance geo-validation work?

When you check in, your browser GPS coordinates are compared against your office location. If you're outside the configured radius (geo threshold), your attendance is flagged as "Geo Mismatch" for HR review. The check-in is still recorded — it is not blocked.

How do I add a new office location?

SuperAdmin only: Go to Locations → Add Location → use the interactive map to set coordinates → save.

You can search for addresses, click on the map, or use "Use My Location" to set GPS coordinates precisely.

How do I configure email (SMTP) settings?

SuperAdmin only: Go to Settings → Mail Configuration → enter SMTP details → Save → Send Test Email to verify.

You'll need your email provider's SMTP server, port, username, and password.

What is the Week-Off Swap auto-cancel feature?

Any pending week-off swap request where the swap date has already passed is automatically cancelled. This runs lazily on API calls and can be triggered manually from Settings → Week-Off Maintenance.

How do I reset the entire system data?

SuperAdmin only: Go to My Profile → Danger Zone → System Data Reset.

This is irreversible. Back up your database before proceeding. All Sevak records, leaves, attendance, and configurations will be permanently deleted.

Can I see attendance reports for all Sevaks?

HR Admin SuperAdmin: Yes. Go to Attendance Reports for compliance reports with geo-mismatch flags. You can filter by date range, department, and export to CSV.

How does the Gurudev's Wisdom feature work?

Every day, a unique quote from Gurudev Sri Sri Ravishankar is displayed on the Dashboard. There are 365 curated quotes — one for each day of the year. No quotes are repeated within a calendar year. The quote changes automatically at midnight.

What happens if I skip HOD approval?

The leave request goes directly to HR for approval. Use this option when the HOD is unavailable (on leave, traveling, etc.) to prevent delays in the approval process. The HOD is still notified for record-keeping.
